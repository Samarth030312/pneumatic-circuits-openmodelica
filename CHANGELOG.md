# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-26)

- Animation in OMEdit's Diagram Window: moving piston and rod, chambers and lines coloured by
  pressure, valves showing their active position, live gauge needles and values, pressed buttons.
- `Learn`: eight lessons (direct and indirect control, OR, AND, speed control, quick exhaust,
  memory valve), three exercises with the parts placed but not connected, a sandbox; every
  component documents its symbol, ports and equation.
- New components: single-acting cylinder, 5/2-way selector-switch valve; a common base for the
  AND and OR valves; `tag` labels with circuit designations.
- `scripts/play.py`: run any circuit with your settings and plot it.
- Trademark and independence notice.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-26)

First release: the `PneumaticCircuits` Modelica library (supply, tube volumes, 3/2 push-button,
selector and roller valves, 5/2 single- and double-pilot valves, two-pressure and shuttle
valves, one-way flow control, quick exhaust, pressure sequence valve, double-acting cylinder,
gauges), three experiments with 48 simulated runs, an independent Python implementation that
checks every run, circuit diagrams drawn from the models, and CI.
